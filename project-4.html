DISCLAIMER

Kindred Goods is a fictional e-commerce brand created as a portfolio project to demonstrate my skills in funnel building, sales copywriting, and checkout optimization. All information, results, statistics, and brand assets presented in this case study are simulated for demonstration purposes.” 

Overview

 A mock sales funnel for Kindred Goods built with GoHighLevel and Funnelish, designed to move a shopper from first click to checkout through a focused, high-converting flow.

The challenge

Standard store pages can distract shoppers with too many choices. The goal was a single-path funnel with persuasive copy, a clear structure, and a smooth checkout, while keeping the brand’s look consistent.

Approach

 I mapped the funnel steps, wrote benefit-led copy, and built pages with a clear headline, social proof, offer details, and focused calls to action. The steps were then connected and tested from start to finish.
