Overview

A mock Shopify store optimization project focused on improving the homepage and product pages to guide visitors toward purchase. The work covers page structure, messaging, trust elements, and mobile-friendly layouts.

The challenge

Visitors often leave when the message is unclear, key benefits are buried, or trust signals are missing. The goal was to present the offer clearly and reduce friction between landing on the store and adding to cart.

Approach

 I reorganized the homepage with a clear headline, benefit-focused sections, customer reviews, and a brand promise. Product pages got stronger titles, benefit bullets, social proof, and clear calls to action. Layouts were checked on desktop and mobile for consistency.
