Overview

A mock inventory tracking and forecasting project built in a spreadsheet to help an online store monitor stock levels, anticipate demand, and plan reorders. It brings SKUs, stock counts, sales, and reorder points into one clear view.

The challenge

Without a clear system, stores can run out of best sellers, overstock slow movers, or reorder too late. The goal was a simple tool that makes stock status and reorder timing easy to see at a glance.

Approach

 I built a stock tracking sheet with SKU details, current stock, reorder points, lead times, and status indicators. Formulas and color coding flag low and out-of-stock items, and a forecast view estimates how long current stock will last and when to reorder.
