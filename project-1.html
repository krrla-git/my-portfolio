DISCLAIMER

Kindred Goods is a fictional e-commerce brand created as a Shopify portfolio project to demonstrate my skills in product merchandising, product-page optimization, SEO copywriting, CRO, and storefront design. All product information, pricing, customer reviews, results, statistics, and brand assets presented in this case study are simulated for demonstration purposes.

Overview

A mock Shopify product optimization project focused on turning basic product information into clean, accurate, and customer-friendly listings. The project covers product setup, optimized copy, imagery, pricing, variants, and essential on-page SEO elements.

The challenge

Raw product information can be inconsistent, unclear, or poorly optimized for shoppers and search engines. The goal was to organize each listing accurately while improving product titles, descriptions, keywords, metadata, and overall page presentation.

Approach

Each product was structured using clear titles, benefit-focused descriptions, key features, organized product data, and optimized SEO fields. Product images and listing details were also reviewed for consistency, creating a polished Shopify product page that reflects a realistic e-commerce workflow.
